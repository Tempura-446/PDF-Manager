ZPDF Phone Companion

Local Wi-Fi transfer — zero cloud, two-way between your phone and desktop ZPDF.

1. Snap Document Pages (Wireless Scanner)

Take one or more photos of paper pages. ZPDF bundles them into a clean PDF and opens it on your computer.

2. Wireless File Drop

Send any PDF, EPUB, CBZ, CBR, or image from your phone straight into ZPDF’s Reader.

3. Grab from Desktop

Download any document currently open in ZPDF on your computer.
